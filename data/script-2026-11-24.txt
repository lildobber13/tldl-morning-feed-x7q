Let's go to the model release round-up, because there's a lot here.

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini. It matters because open-weight models are getting seriously capable at small sizes. It's a 26.4 billion parameter model that does extra internal computation to reason more before it answers. It leads every open-weight model under 100 billion parameters on the DeepSWE coding benchmark.

Tencent released Hy4, also open-weight. It handles text, image, and video understanding, with a 770 billion parameter backbone that activates 49 billion parameters per token. It brings a million-token context window, sparse attention, and a speculative-decoding drafter. Tencent is aiming it at coding, computer use, finance, research, and long-running assistant workflows.

NVIDIA has a different approach with Nemotron-Labs-Sigma. It's a family of 14 open checkpoints at 3 billion and 8 billion parameters. Instead of predicting one token at a time, these models generate text by denoising compact token embeddings. Why care? Speed. The 8 billion model reaches a 3.46 times average throughput speedup over standard autoregressive decoding on a GB200.
