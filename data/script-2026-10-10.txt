Let's go under the hood for a minute. One explainer covers why speculative decoding is fast. A small draft model proposes a likely sequence, and the big model checks it in parallel in one pass. When batch sizes are small, that uses compute that would otherwise sit idle. At high throughput, though, you can waste compute that you'd rather spend moving memory. Neat tradeoff. Elsewhere, a benchmark called ATLAS tests search agents on real-world tasks that need web searches. Even high-effort agents miss a lot of golden answers, so search has real room to improve.
