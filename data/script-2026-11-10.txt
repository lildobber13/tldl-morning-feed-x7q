Fourth, science, which has some real highlights. A research system at PSI, built on an Anthropic model, has proven the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture, a key open problem in quantum complexity theory. The result shows that quantum computers aren't exponentially faster than classical ones on random inputs, which settles a big question about quantum speedups.

In medicine, researchers developed an mRNA therapy that wakes up heart muscle cells so they divide and help repair damage. In mice, it produced stronger heartbeats after a heart attack. It's not a cure for heart disease, but even small gains in heart function have been tied to lower mortality. The effect is short-lived and localized, and it isn't expected to permanently change the heart cells. It needs trials in larger animals before humans.
