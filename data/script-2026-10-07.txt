OpenAI also put its Decisions API into public beta. It takes text and images and returns one of three things: a probability that a condition is true, a choice from a fixed set, or a score against a rubric. They say it's up to ten times faster than GPT six Luna through the Responses API. It costs ten cents per million input tokens, with no charges for cache reads, cache writes, or output. The use cases are things like classifying content, routing requests, and prioritizing work. And there's a fun follow up. One writer is calling it the decision model gold rush. Within a day of launch, TypeSafe's Jev decision model was wired into 13 percent of Vercel's paid AI Gateway customer workflows. Within three days Cloudflare, LangChain, and Langfuse had shipped integrations, and competitors took about two weeks to show up. The post walks through how to pick one for production.
