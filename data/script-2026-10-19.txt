But that same scale makes security more complicated. Modern applications can depend on hundreds or thousands of components through deep dependency trees. So understanding risk takes more than counting vulnerabilities. How many were disclosed, how many were critical, how many packages were affected. Those numbers matter, but they don't answer the bigger question of real exposure. A critical vulnerability might pose little practical risk in your environment. A lower severity weakness might become serious when it's paired with another one. Compromised credentials or permissive settings can change the picture too. Finding the vulnerability is just the start. Teams still have to understand the risk, decide if action is needed, find a fix, and get it to the software that needs it.
